# 🎬 SAFARSETU — OFFICIAL VIDEO PRODUCTION SCRIPT & STORYBOARD
**Languages**: 1. हिन्दी (Hindi) | 2. English | 3. निमाड़ी (Nimadi)  
**Target Duration**: 60 - 90 Seconds (Ideal for Instagram Reels, YouTube Ads, WhatsApp & Facebook Promo)  
**Format**: 9:16 (Vertical Reel) & 16:9 (Horizontal Video)

## 📋 SCENE-BY-SCENE PRODUCTION BLUEPRINT

### 🎬 SCENE 1: THE HOOK & EVERYDAY PROBLEM (00:00 - 00:10)
* **Visual**: 
  - Ek vyakti sadak par khada hai, baar-baar haath ki ghadi dekh raha hai. 
  - Pichhe se bheed-bhaad wali bus nikal rahi hai ya auto mana kar raha hai. 
  - Screen par text pop: *"Cab nahi mil rahi? Gaadi ka jhanjhat?"*
* **Sound Effect / BGM**: Thoda tension bhara, halka sa comic tick-tock sound.

#### 🎙️ Voiceover (VO):
- **हिन्दी (Hindi)**:  
  *"Emergency me gaadi nahi mil rahi? Bus ka lamba intezar aur manmaana kiraya? Ab chinta chhodiye!"*
- **English**:  
  *"Stuck on the road waiting for a cab? High fares and endless delays? Not anymore!"*
- **निमाड़ी (Nimadi)**:  
  *"राम राम दादा! कड़े जावा छे? इंदौर, खंडवा के खरगोन? धूप-बारिश मा बस-गाड़ी का चक्कर मा हैरान-परेशान होइ रया? अब कतरोई दूर जाणो होय, चिंता बिल्कुल नी करणी!"*

## 🎙️ RECORDING & PROMO TIPS FOR SOCIAL MEDIA
1. **Instagram Reels & YouTube Shorts (30-60 sec Cut)**:
   - Use Scene 1 (Hook), Scene 2 (Cars), Scene 4 (3 steps + AM/PM), and Scene 7 (CTA).
2. **Local WhatsApp Status / Village Community Groups**:
   - Nimadi voiceover script will go viral in local districts (Khargone, Barwani, Khandwa, Burhanpur, Dhar, Indore) because it connects with the local culture and language!
3. **Voice Artist Direction**:
   - **Hindi**: Energetic, warm, friendly Indian commercial tone.
   - **English**: Confident, crisp, tech-startup accent.
   - **Nimadi**: ठेठ, आदरपूर्ण, और देसी आवाज़ (जैसे अपने गाँव का कोई समझदार और चहेता भाई बात कर रहा हो).
